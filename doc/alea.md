# alea

![alea](../img/alea.png)

*alea* adds a single random module to your rack, chosen from your entire library. Inspired by [WhatTheRack from korfuri](https://github.com/korfuri/WhatTheRack).

## How to use

Click the ⚂ (die) button. A module is picked at random from all models available in your library and added to the rack next to *alea*.
